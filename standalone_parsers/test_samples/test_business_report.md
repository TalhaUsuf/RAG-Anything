# Quarterly Business Report

## Executive Summary

This report presents the quarterly revenue analysis for fiscal year 2025.
Revenue has shown consistent growth across all four quarters.

![Quarterly Revenue Chart](chart.png)

## Detailed Financial Data

| Quarter | Revenue ($M) | Costs ($M) | Profit ($M) | Margin |
|---------|-------------|------------|-------------|--------|
| Q1 2025 | 120 | 84 | 36 | 30% |
| Q2 2025 | 180 | 117 | 63 | 35% |
| Q3 2025 | 150 | 105 | 45 | 30% |
| Q4 2025 | 210 | 131 | 79 | 38% |
| **Total** | **660** | **437** | **223** | **34%** |

## Growth Analysis

The compound quarterly growth rate can be expressed as:

$$CQGR = \left(\frac{Revenue_{Q4}}{Revenue_{Q1}}\right)^{\frac{1}{3}} - 1 = \left(\frac{210}{120}\right)^{0.333} - 1 \approx 20.5\%$$

This indicates a strong upward trend in revenue performance.
